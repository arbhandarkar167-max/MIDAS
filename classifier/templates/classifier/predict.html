Clinical Best Practices

  • Center the lesion in the frame, occupying at least 50% of the field.
  • Use dermoscopic immersion or polarization to resolve pigment nets.
  • Avoid harsh flash reflections, blurry focus, or peripheral casting.
  • Accurately report lesion length and width in millimeters.
  • Record patient's true Fitzpatrick skin phototype for calibrated priors.
